Prompt variants used in the study.
The main analysis uses main_prompt.

[main_prompt]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale, considering (i) standardisation and text/data-processing intensity, (ii) site presence, coordination, and regulatory compliance, and (iii) creative judgement, interdisciplinary decision-making, and face-to-face communication.

[no_standardisation]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale, considering site presence, coordination, regulatory compliance, creative judgement, interdisciplinary decision-making, and face-to-face communication. Do not use text/data-processing intensity or standardisation as explicit scoring criteria.

[no_site_regulation]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale, considering standardisation, text/data-processing intensity, creative judgement, interdisciplinary decision-making, and face-to-face communication. Do not use site presence, coordination, or regulatory compliance as explicit scoring criteria.

[no_creativity_communication]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale, considering standardisation, text/data-processing intensity, site presence, coordination, and regulatory compliance. Do not use creative judgement, interdisciplinary decision-making, or face-to-face communication as explicit scoring criteria.

[neutral_zero_shot]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale. Use your best judgement without applying any provided weighting criteria.

[reordered_criteria]
Rate the generative-AI exposure of the occupation on a 0-5 integer scale, considering (i) creative judgement, interdisciplinary decision-making, and face-to-face communication, (ii) site presence, coordination, and regulatory compliance, and (iii) standardisation and text/data-processing intensity.
